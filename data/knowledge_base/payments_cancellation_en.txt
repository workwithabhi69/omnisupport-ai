# E-Commerce Payment Methods & Order Cancellation Policy (Demo Retailer)
# Language: English
# Category: Payment Methods & Order Cancellation

## Accepted Payment Methods
- UPI: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and any bank UPI ID.
- Cards: Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, and American Express credit & debit cards.
- Net Banking: Supported for over 50 leading Indian banks.
- Cash on Delivery (COD): Available for orders up to ₹10,000 for verified delivery pin codes.
- EMI: No-Cost EMI and low-cost EMI available on eligible credit cards and select debit cards.

## Order Cancellation Rules
- You can cancel your order free of charge at any time before it enters the "Dispatched" state.
- To cancel an order: Navigate to "My Orders", click on the specific order, and select "Cancel Order".
- If the order has already been dispatched from our facility, it cannot be cancelled online. However, you can reject the delivery when the courier arrives at your doorstep.
- For prepaid cancelled orders, 100% full refund is issued instantly back to your original payment method.
